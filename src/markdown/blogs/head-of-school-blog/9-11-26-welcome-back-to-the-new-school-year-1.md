Hello, CTL Families, 



What a joyful and connected start to the year this week has been. Thank you for all that you do to support CTL, your kiddos, our amazing teachers, and everyone in the community. 



While CTL is a vibrant and loving place, we acknowledge that it is not a neighborhood school and most kids can’t ride their bikes or walk over to a friend from CTL’s house after school or on the weekends. CTL families have been talking about ways to help our community feel a little closer. Last year, a group of 10-15 interested parents and Anne and I began meeting and calling our idea group the Village. 



This year, we’ll be launching two initiatives meant to help the school be closer. Our hope is that these new additions will support making connections for both students and parents/caregivers outside of the classroom; they are intended to further strengthen the CTL community.



The first is that one parent in each grade (two per class) have volunteered to be the Class Connectors. These parents will plan a meet up for the class outside of school about once a month. Attend when you can and know that everyone is invited! At the Curriculum Afternoons over the next two weeks, you can expect your classroom teacher to introduce the Class Connectors. 



We’re also launching “opt in” app participation in BAND, which will offer a chance for everyone in the class to let others know when they are doing activities where friends might join. Expect to hear more about how you can join BAND in an upcoming email from the Class Connectors, coming within the next two weeks. 



CTL’s Village continues to meet, with our next meeting on Wednesday, September 30 from 3:00-4:15. The group is open to everyone and there is free aftercare during the meeting. Register with Sarah for aftercare and to receive the agenda ahead of time or just come if the opportunity arises! Please join us.



One place that we hope to see lots of CTL families is at the Bath Book Bash next week. On Saturday the 19th, CTL will sponsor the Book Bash Character Costume Contest and Parade, where people dress up as book characters. CTL will have costumes available for borrowing at the event, for the parade or just for fun! We will take a CTL picture at our “Step Into a Book” tent at 11:30, before the parade at noon, and again just after the costume awards are announced. Come by the tent anytime between 11:00 and 4:00!



We hope that these initiatives will serve to bring us all closer together. If you are seeking community, come. 



Take care and keep in touch.



Love, Katy
